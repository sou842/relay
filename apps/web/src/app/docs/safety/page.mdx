# Staying safe

Relay links to WhatsApp as an **unofficial client**. WhatsApp's terms do not
allow unofficial clients, and accounts that look automated can be banned.

## Lower the risk

- Use a **secondary or test number** you can afford to lose.
- Message people who know you and expect your messages.
- Keep a human pace. The default send delay spaces API messages out.
- Avoid sending the same text to many people, and avoid cold outreach.
- Don't link a brand-new number and immediately send lots of messages.

## Not supported

Relay is not for bulk or unsolicited messaging, and it does not include
anything to get around WhatsApp's spam detection.

## Official alternative

For business messaging at scale, use Meta's official **WhatsApp Business
Platform (Cloud API)**, directly or through a provider. It needs business
verification and approved templates, but your number is not at risk.
